Campaign process

How Our Advertising Campaigns Work

From registration to campaign completion, every stage is designed to support transparent, compliant and effective mobile advertising.

01

Create Your Profile

Complete the registration form with your basic information.

02

Complete KYC

Submit the required identification documents and selfie verification where applicable.

03

Review & Approval

Submitted information is reviewed before participation in an advertising campaign.

04

Campaign Matching

Eligible participants may be matched with suitable advertising campaigns based on campaign requirements.

05

Receive Advertising Material

Approved campaign materials and installation instructions are provided.

06

Display & Drive

Participants display the approved advertisement and follow the campaign's requirements.

07

Campaign Completion

Campaign activity is monitored according to the applicable campaign terms.
