About us

Advertising That Moves With People

RouteWave connects advertisers with approved vehicle owners and mobile campaign participants to bring brand messages into everyday movement, communities and urban activity.

Our mission

Creative advertising in motion

To help brands reach audiences through creative, mobile advertising while creating opportunities for eligible vehicle owners and campaign participants.

Our approach

Our campaigns may involve cars, vans, buses, trucks, motorcycles and other approved moving platforms depending on project requirements, campaign eligibility and local operating conditions.

Campaign requirements vary by advertiser, target audience, region and campaign objective. Each opportunity is reviewed to ensure compliance with the relevant terms and brand standards.

Why mobile advertising?

Reach beyond the static screen

Greater Visibility

Mobile advertising follows real routes where people live, work and travel, increasing everyday exposure.

Real-World Reach

Messages move through city streets, commercial districts and community routes that fixed media cannot cover as effectively.

Flexible Campaigns

Campaigns can be designed around different vehicle types, routes, markets and promotional objectives.
